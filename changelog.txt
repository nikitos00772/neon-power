Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-11-24
  Features:
    - Cables are a bright core inside a coloured tube, with a soft outer shine that fades out. Unlit cables are a grey tube.
    - New map settings: cable tube width, outer shine smoothness, and a separate pole light at night.
  Changes:
    - Cables follow the vanilla wire curve exactly and are drawn over the vanilla copper wire, so wire previews show when building poles.
    - Neon cables run only between two neon poles; a wire from a neon pole to a vanilla pole stays a plain copper wire and draws no power.
    - The cable glow strength setting was removed and the light settings were renamed, so those settings are back to their (new) defaults.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-22
  Features:
    - Neon versions of the small, medium and big electric pole and the substation, all unlocked by the Neon power technology.

  Changes:
    - Cables (try to) follow the vanilla wire shape.
    - Each lit cable draws a fixed 1 kW.
